    How it works

    1. A customer orders and pays your UPI ID directly. The site never touches the money.
    2. The order shows under Awaiting payment check. Check your UPI app for the payment.
    3. Approve it to see the customer's name, room and number. Make it, deliver it, mark it delivered.
    4. Fake or unpaid? Tap Not received or Flag fake.

    Problem with an order?
